Privacy policy

This policy explains what information SP IT Solutions collects through this website and how we handle it, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Information we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect the name, contact details, business name, and message you choose to provide. We do not collect payment or ID information through this website.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, or deliver a service you've asked for. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Analytics and cookies

We may use basic, privacy-friendly analytics to understand how visitors use this site (for example, which pages are viewed). This does not identify you personally. You can decline non-essential cookies via the notice shown when you first visit the site.

Information Officer

SP IT Solutions will appoint an Information Officer responsible for POPIA compliance as the business is formally registered. Contact details for the Information Officer will be published here once appointed. In the meantime, direct any data-related queries to hello@spitsolutions.co.za.

Data retention

We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed to respond to you or deliver an agreed service, unless we're required to keep it longer for legal or accounting reasons.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@spitsolutions.co.za.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the business grows and formalises its compliance processes. The latest version will always be available on this page.
