About us

A small, accountable team — on purpose

SP IT Solutions is founder-led. That's not a stopgap until we "grow up" into an agency — it's how we keep pricing honest and communication direct.

Our mission

To give small and medium businesses access to professional, affordable, and trustworthy IT expertise that's normally only available to larger companies — helping them build, secure, and maintain the technology they depend on.

Our vision

To grow into a recognised, trusted regional IT partner — known for combining strong technical development skills with dependable, everyday IT support.

Values

What we hold ourselves to

Reliability

Showing up, meeting deadlines, and honouring what we agreed to.

Affordability

Priced for the SMME market we actually serve, not a corporate budget.

Continuous learning

Staying current through ongoing training and certification.

Integrity

Being transparent about scope, cost and capability — especially in security work.
